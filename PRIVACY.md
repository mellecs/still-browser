# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026 · App version: 2.2.0 · [Versión en español](PRIVACIDAD.md)_

> **In short:** Still Browser has no accounts, does not send your history to any server run by the developer, and includes no analytics. Your data stays on your computer. The connections the browser makes are the ones you trigger by browsing, plus the third-party services explained below.

## 1. Who is responsible

Still Browser is a desktop browser developed by Melle (GitHub: [mellecs](https://github.com/mellecs)). For any privacy question, write to [aaronmellewebs@gmail.com] or open an issue at [github.com/mellecs/still-browser/issues](https://github.com/mellecs/still-browser/issues).

## 2. Data stored on your computer

To work, the browser stores the following data in your system's application data folder. **None of it is sent to the developer.**

- Browsing history.
- Settings and the theme you chose.
- Tabs from your last session, so they can be restored.
- Your download list (name, source address, path and status).
- Permissions you grant or deny to each website.
- Your shortcuts on the start page.
- Cookies, local storage and cache from the sites you visit, as in any browser.

You can delete your history and browsing data from within the browser. If you uninstall the app and delete its data folder, nothing remains.

## 3. What we do not do

- There is no user account or sign-up.
- We do not collect usage data, telemetry or analytics.
- We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have it.
- We do not show advertising.

## 4. Third-party connections

Although the developer does not receive your data, the browser connects to the services below, which have their own privacy policies:

- **The websites you visit.** They receive your IP address and the usual data any browser sends. What they do with it depends on each site.
- **DuckDuckGo.** It is the default search engine: whatever you type in the address bar to search is sent to DuckDuckGo.
- **GitHub.** The app checks for new versions by querying the repository's releases. GitHub may log your IP address during that request.
- **Chrome Web Store.** If you install or update extensions from the store, the browser connects to Google's servers.
- **Ad blocker filter lists.** The ad blocker may download updated lists from external servers.
- **Extensions you install.** Each extension is made by a third party and may collect data. Review their permissions before installing them.

## 5. Permissions and downloads

Camera, microphone, location and notifications are only enabled if a site asks and you accept. You can change that decision later. Files you download are saved to the folder you choose and are not analyzed or uploaded anywhere.

## 6. Children

Still Browser is not aimed at children under 14 and does not collect data from anyone. Minors should use it under adult supervision.

## 7. Your rights

Because the developer does not store your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our servers. Everything the browser stores is on your computer, and you can view and delete it at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es)).

## 8. Changes to this policy

If something important changes, we will update this page and the date above. We recommend checking it from time to time.
